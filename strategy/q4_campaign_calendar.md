## 1. The Q4 US Ad Market Reality & Seasonality Dynamics

Q4 in the United States is the most competitive and expensive ad season of the entire year:
* **CPM Inflation**: Meta and Google ad CPMs typically spike by **35% to 55%** between mid-November and Cyber Monday due to e-commerce and retail brands bidding aggressively.
* **The Counter-Strategy for EdTech**: E-commerce brands push physical products. EdTech wins in Q4 by positioning education as **The Ultimate Meaningful Gift** and capturing **Winter Break parent availability** and **New Year academic resolutions**.
* **Counselor Shift Management**: During Thanksgiving (Nov 26–27) and Christmas (Dec 24–25), demo show rates plummet if parents are traveling. Our ad scheduling dynamically re-allocates budget away from those dead travel days into high-intent prep periods.

## 3. Counselor Shift & Timezone Routing Strategy in Q4

As proven in our Round 1 analysis, routing American parents to their local evening hours (`US_SHIFT`) was **statistically significant ($z = 2.65, p = 0.0081$)** and boosted conversion from **7.6% to 11.8%**.

In Q4, we align counselor schedules across all 4 US timezones:
1. **Eastern Time (EST - NYC, Atlanta, Boston, Miami)**: Peak demo hours = 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM EST (2:30 AM – 6:30 AM IST / Dedicated US Shift).
2. **Central Time (CST - Texas, Chicago)**: Peak demo hours = 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM CST.
3. **Pacific Time (PST - California, Washington)**: Peak demo hours = 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM PST.
4. **Winter Break Day Shifts**: Between Dec 18 and Jan 3, we add day slots (11:00 AM – 3:00 PM local US time) because children are on school vacation.
