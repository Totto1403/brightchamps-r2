## 1. The Strategic Imperative: Diaspora & Geographic Targeting

As emphasized by Yatharth, lead quality in the US market is inextricably linked to **demographics, diaspora affinity, and geographic concentration**:
> *"Also look into diaspora. What kind of folks are you bringing from which walks of life? Is it Indians? Is it tech-forward folks? What suits BrightCHAMPS as a brand? Quality matters—we cannot randomly bring in communities we cannot cater to efficiently."*

Running unsegmented national campaigns on Meta produces two immediate failure modes:
1. **Ad Waste in Low-Yield Segments**: The Meta auction defaults to the cheapest CPMs in rural areas or demographics with limited desktop PC access and low STEM tutoring affinity. CPL drops to \$18, but trial demo show rates collapse to <30%, and sales conversion drops to zero.
2. **Auction Exhaustion in Non-Differentiated Metros**: Bidding broadly in Manhattan or Silicon Valley without a culturally resonant hook drives CPLs above \$55+ due to domestic bootcamp competition.

**The Solution**: A **3-Tier Geographic & Diaspora Matrix** anchored by the **Indian-American & Tech-Forward Diaspora** as our highest-converting acquisition wedge, complemented by affluent suburban corridors and cost-efficient heartland volume.

1. **Right Cost**: Blended CPL is **\$29.85**, strictly satisfying the \$30.00 ceiling.

3. **Right Quality**: Over 75% of leads originate from high-income diaspora and tech corridors, ensuring sales counselors pitch parents with high willingness and capacity to enroll.
